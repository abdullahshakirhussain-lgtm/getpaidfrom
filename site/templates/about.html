Where answers come from

Service pages, government sites, central banks and tax offices first. Guides and news only when official pages say nothing, and then the line says it rests on one source.

The short answer

For clients paying you, we name the first that works in this order: PayPal, Payoneer, Wise. For selling online: Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, Polar, Paddle, Stripe. Same rule for every country.

Legitimate routes only

We never suggest borrowed accounts, foreign addresses or VPN tricks. They break the services' rules, get money frozen and can break the law.

Privacy

No cookies, no ad trackers, no IP addresses kept. We count page views by page and by visitor country (the country comes from the network, not from you), so we know which pages people need. That's all we record about visits.

Email alerts. If you sign up, we keep your email address and the countries you picked, only to email you when those pages change. We never share or sell it. To stop, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or the form below.
