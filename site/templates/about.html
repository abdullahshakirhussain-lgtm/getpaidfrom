How we check

Every answer on this site links to the sources it comes from, with the date we checked them. If we could not find a reliable source, the answer stays blank instead of being guessed.

What the badges mean

  • Verified At least one official source (a government, central bank, tax authority, or the platform's own page), or two independent secondary sources that agree.
  • Single source Only one secondary source, for example a tax firm's guide or a news article. Usable as a lead, but confirm it before relying on it.
  • Sources disagree Sources conflict. We show both sides and, where possible, what the difference means for you in practice.
  • Not yet researched We could not find or reach a reliable source. Many small states publish little online, and some government sites block automated access or have expired security certificates.

How a source is checked

  1. We find the page through search, a sitemap or an official index, never by guessing addresses.
  2. We save a copy of the page with its capture date, so the answer can be traced even if the page later changes.
  3. Each answer quotes the exact sentence that supports it. An automated check confirms that every quote still matches the saved copy.
  4. Key figures (rates, thresholds) need an official source or two independent sources before an answer is marked verified.
  5. We never bypass bot checks, logins or broken security certificates. Those pages are recorded as blocked.

Platform lists

The platform table on each country page comes from each platform's published country list (Stripe, PayPal, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Whop, Upwork, Etsy, YouTube, Google Play). Being on a list means the platform says it supports your country. It does not guarantee your own account will be approved.

Workarounds

The "Simple legal routes" section only suggests legitimate options, such as merchant-of-record platforms, local official channels and bank transfers. It never suggests using someone else's account, a foreign address or a VPN to look like you are somewhere else. Those breach platform terms, get funds frozen and can break anti-money-laundering or sanctions law. For sanctioned countries we say plainly when there is no clean route.

Not advice

This is general information, not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules change, so check the linked source or a qualified local adviser before you act.
