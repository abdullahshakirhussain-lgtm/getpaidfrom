# Logo sources

Logos are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify each service. Get Paid From is not affiliated with any of them.

- paypal, payoneer, wise, gumroad, lemon-squeezy, payhip, paddle, stripe, upwork: Simple Icons 16.36.0 (CC0-1.0 for the SVG files; the marks stay trademarks), https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.36.0/icons/
- polar: the mark from https://polar.sh (site header SVG, fetched 2026-10-26); Polar also publishes https://polar.sh/assets/brand/polar_brand.zip

Colours: `colors.json` holds each brand's official hex from the same Simple Icons release (simple-icons.json). Polar has no colour there and its own site draws the mark in black, so it stays ink. Colours below 3:1 contrast on white (Wise, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Paddle) are kept exactly and shown on a small ink square instead of being darkened.
